I am Tran Nguyen To Tam, a final-year Multimedia Communication Management student at FPT University, Ho Chi Minh City. My work sits at the intersection of strategic communication, ethical storytelling, and community engagement.

From planning media strategies at VNExpress to managing crisis communication for a high-visibility university showcase, I bring both analytical thinking and creative sensibility to every project. I believe the most impactful communication is rooted in empathy — understanding the audience before crafting the message.

Beyond the professional sphere, I am passionate about social impact: I have led fundraising campaigns for children's charities, contributed to gender education initiatives, and advocated for reading culture across Binh Phuoc Province.

My long-term goal is to become a communication strategist who builds sustainable brand reputations grounded in ethical values and social responsibility.
